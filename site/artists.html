What you'll need

  • A free Cloudflare account. Cloudflare hosts your music, for free. That covers about 250 songs with no card at all. For more room (about a thousand songs), Cloudflare asks you to add a card, though it's still free.
  • A way to be paid. One tap with Face ID makes a payout wallet: the account your fans' payments land in. To move it to your bank, cash out to Coinbase (most countries) or Cash App (US) from your editor. Card subscriptions through your own Stripe account are optional.
  • Your own listeners. Amply doesn't promote anyone or run a chart. You share your link with the people who already listen to you.
