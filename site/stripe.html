How subscriptions work

For artists

Card payments, through your own Stripe

Let fans subscribe by card, or buy your songs and albums to keep. The money goes straight to your Stripe account, and you can try it all with pretend money first.

Before you start

  • It's your Stripe account, not ours. Stripe pays you directly and takes its usual fee (about 2.9% + 30¢ a payment in the US). Amply takes nothing and never sees the money.
  • To test, you need only an email address. Real payments need your ID and a bank account, as an individual or a company.
  • Your key stays on your streaming service. It can do only the few things Amply needs, and it's never shown again, to you or to Amply.

1. Make a Stripe account

Sign up at dashboard.stripe.com/register. You can skip Stripe's setup questions for now: every account starts in test mode, where nothing real is charged.

Set the name fans will see on Stripe's checkout page to your artist name, with a support email: Settings → Public details.

2. Get a key for Amply

With Amply for Artists (one click)

  1. Install Amply for Artists in your Stripe account. Stripe shows what the key can do; choose Install.
  2. Choose View API keys and copy the key (it starts rk_test_ in test mode).

Or by hand

  1. Open API keys in test mode, create a restricted key, and choose Building your own integration.
  2. Set exactly these, and leave every other row as None:
    RowSet toWhy
    ProductsWriteto make what you sell
    Payment LinksWritea checkout link for each plan, song and album
    PricesWriteto set their prices
    Customer portalWriteso fans can cancel on Stripe's own page
    SubscriptionsReadto confirm a subscription is paid
    Checkout SessionsReadto check which fan a payment was for
    The key can't move money, see card numbers, issue refunds or pay anything out.
  3. Create it and copy it. It starts rk_test_; Stripe shows it only once, so if you lose it, make another.

3. Connect it

  1. In your editor, open Settings → Subscriptions.
  2. For subscriptions, tick the plans you want and set their prices (each at least $2). Only selling songs and albums? Choose Connect for music only instead, and put prices on your albums and songs under Music.
  3. Paste the key and connect. Your streaming service sets everything up in your Stripe account.
  4. Publish. Plans and prices appear in the Amply app.

If a permission is missing, connecting says which, and undoes anything it had already made. Fix the key in Stripe and connect again.

4. Try it

  1. Open your page in the Amply app and tap a plan.
  2. Save the backup key the app shows you, then continue to Stripe.
  3. Pay with Stripe's test card: 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry date, any three digits, any name and postcode.
  4. You land back in the app, subscribed, and your paid tracks play. In your editor, under Listeners, you appear as a subscriber.
  5. To test cancelling: on your page in the app, choose Manage or cancel.

5. Go live

  1. Activate your Stripe account. In the Stripe dashboard, follow Activate payments: your details, ID and bank account. Stripe sometimes takes a day or two to check.
  2. Get a live key. Switch off test mode and get a key the same way as in step 2 (the app, or by hand with the same six permissions). A live key starts rk_live_.
  3. Swap it in. In your editor, under Subscriptions, choose Switch to a live key, paste it, and save.
  4. Publish. From now on, payments are real.

Test subscribers don't carry over — test mode and live mode are separate worlds in Stripe.

Once it's running

  • Getting paid. Stripe pays out to your bank on a schedule you set in the Stripe dashboard, usually every few days.
  • Cancelling. Fans cancel from your page in the Amply app, on Stripe's own page; they keep listening until the end of what they paid for. You can also cancel anyone from Subscriptions in the Stripe dashboard.
  • Refunds are up to you, from the payment in the Stripe dashboard.
  • Receipts. Turn on emailed receipts in Stripe under Settings → Customer emails, so fans get one for each payment and renewal.
  • Tax. Selling subscriptions makes you a seller. In the EU that usually means VAT and a 14-day right to cancel; some US states tax digital music. Stripe Tax can work it out for about 0.5% a payment. We can't give tax advice — if you're unsure, ask an accountant.
  • Changing prices makes new checkout links. People already subscribed keep the price they signed up at.
  • Stopping. Stop selling subscriptions takes the plans off your page. People already subscribed keep listening, and keep being renewed by Stripe, until they cancel; your streaming service lets go of your key once the last one ends.

If something goes wrong

"Your key is missing a permission…"
It names the row. In Stripe, open the ⋯ menu next to your key → Edit key, set that row, save, and connect again.
"That's Stripe's publishable key"
Stripe shows two keys together. The publishable one (pk_) is public and no use here; copy the secret one below it, which starts rk_. You may need to choose Reveal first.
"Stripe doesn't recognise that key"
Copy it again — the whole thing, starting rk_. If you've lost it, make a new one; Stripe only shows a key once.
"Your Stripe account isn't activated for real payments yet"
Finish Activate payments in the Stripe dashboard, or keep using a test key until you have.
A fan paid but isn't shown as subscribed
It can take a minute. If it still hasn't appeared, the fan can choose Check again on your page in the app — that finds a payment even if they never made it back from Stripe. Check your streaming service is up to date, too (the editor offers updates).
"Your key couldn't set up Stripe's cancellation page"
Add Customer portal — Write to the key and save your plans again. Fans must be able to cancel as easily as they subscribed.
