Paying for music

Never used crypto? Start here.

Artists on Amply are paid as you listen, straight from your wallet to theirs, with nobody taking a cut. Setting that up takes about ten minutes, once.

Two ways to pay an artist

Each artist chooses, and their page in the app says which:

  • By subscription — pay by card for a year or a few months. No crypto needed: tap Subscribe on their page, save the backup key the app shows you, and pay on Stripe's page. You can stop reading here.
  • By the minute — the app pays a fraction of a cent for each minute you listen, from a small wallet on your phone. That needs the setup below, once.

Free tracks play for anyone, either way.

Setting up paying by the minute

It takes a couple of minutes, once. The Amply app keeps a small wallet on your phone, you put a few dollars in it by card, and it pays artists as you listen.

  1. Make your wallet. Open the Amply app at amply.stream/app, tap You, then Make my wallet. Then tap Save next to Backup key and keep that key somewhere safe, like a password manager. It's the only way to get your money back if you lose your phone.
  2. Add money by card. Tap Top up, then an amount: $20 lasts months of everyday listening. A page opens showing your wallet address. Tap Copy address and continue, and Stripe's checkout opens. Choose Solana as the network, paste the address when it asks, and pay. Stripe may ask for ID the first time. That's normal, and required by law.
  3. Add a little SOL, once. If the app asks, tap Add SOL and buy about $1 of it the same way. SOL pays the network's fee, a fraction of a cent each time, so a dollar lasts thousands of payments.
  4. Listen. Add an artist by their link or their website. You'll see their price before anything is charged, and the app pays them as you listen. There's nothing to approve each time.

Already use a crypto wallet like Phantom? You can send USDC and a little SOL from it instead: tap Top up, then Or send it from Phantom. Always use the Solana network, and send USDC itself, not Phantom's "Cash".

What it costs you

  • Adding money: Stripe's fee, roughly 1.5% plus 30¢ each time, so about 60¢ on $20. Stripe shows the exact amount before you pay. Topping up $20 once costs less than $5 four times.
  • Listening: about a tenth of a cent each time the app pays an artist. It's a fixed amount, not a percentage, so ten hours of listening in a month costs about 5¢ in fees.

The artist receives every cent of their price. Amply takes nothing.

Getting your money back

In the Amply app, tap You, then Send back. Paste the Solana address of any wallet you control (in Phantom: Receive, choose Solana, copy), and send. Whatever you haven't spent goes back to you.

If you're an artist

  1. Install Phantom and create a wallet, as above. Write down the words. You don't need to buy anything to be paid.
  2. Give Amply your address. In your editor, open Settings → Who gets paid. On a computer with the Phantom browser extension, tap Use my Phantom address; otherwise, in Phantom tap Receive, choose Solana, copy it, and paste it in.
  3. Publish. Payments arrive in Phantom as people listen.

More on choosing a wallet, and on getting money into your bank, is in Which wallet should I use? To let fans subscribe by card as well, see Card subscriptions with Stripe.

Staying safe

  • The words are the money. Nobody from Amply, Phantom or anywhere else will ever need them. Anyone who asks is trying to steal from you.
  • Always Solana. If an app or exchange asks which network to send on, the answer is Solana. USDC sent any other way won't arrive, and usually can't be recovered.
  • Keep it small. Treat the Amply wallet like cash in a pocket: a few dollars at a time. The app will never send more than $2 in one payment or $10 in a day, whatever an artist's page asks for.
