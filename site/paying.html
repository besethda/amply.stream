Paying for music

Never used crypto? Start here.

Artists on Amply are paid as you listen, straight from your wallet to theirs, with nobody taking a cut. Setting that up takes a few minutes, once.

Two ways to pay an artist

Each artist chooses, and their page in the app says which:

  • By subscription — pay by card for a year or a few months. No crypto needed: tap Subscribe on their page and pay on Stripe's page. The app makes a wallet first, only as your name, so the subscription can follow you to a new phone. You can stop reading here.
  • By the minute — the app pays a fraction of a cent for each minute you listen, from a small wallet on your phone. That needs the setup below, once.

Free tracks play for anyone, either way.

Setting up paying by the minute

It takes a few minutes, once. The Amply app keeps a small wallet for you, you put a few dollars in it, and it pays artists as you listen.

  1. Make your wallet. Open the Amply app at amply.stream/app, tap You, then Make my wallet with Face ID or a passkey. On a phone that's Face ID or your fingerprint. On a computer it's Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your phone: the browser shows a QR code to scan. There are no recovery words: your passkey is the key, and it syncs to your other devices, where I've used Amply before opens the same wallet. If your device can't do this, choose Use a backup code instead and keep that code somewhere safe, like a password manager.
  2. Add money. Tap Top up and pick whichever you already use:
    • Coinbase (where the app offers it): opens Coinbase with everything filled in. You need a Coinbase account (free).
    • Cash App (US): copy your wallet's address, then in Cash App go to Pay, paste it in the search box, and pay. Cash App sends it as digital dollars (USDC) on the Solana network. Not available in New York.
    • Card: Stripe's checkout. Paste your address when it asks and choose Solana as the network. Stripe may ask for ID the first time; that's normal, and required by law. Stripe doesn't sell digital dollars on Solana in the EU yet, so in Europe use Coinbase, or send from a crypto wallet as below.
  3. Add $1 for fees, once. If the app asks, tap Add $1 for fees. Each payment needs a tiny network fee, paid in a currency called SOL, and a dollar covers thousands of payments.
  4. Listen. Add an artist by their link or their website. You'll see their price before anything is charged, and the app pays them as you listen. There's nothing to approve each time.

Already use a crypto wallet like Phantom? You can send USDC and a little SOL from it instead: tap Top up, then Already use a crypto wallet? Send from it instead. Always use the Solana network, and send USDC itself, not Phantom's "Cash".

What it costs you

  • Adding money: it depends on how. Coinbase charges about 0.5% from a bank and 2.5% by card. Cash App is free for now, as an offer of theirs. Card through Stripe costs whatever Stripe shows before you pay. Each of them shows the exact amount before you confirm. Topping up less often, in larger amounts, costs less in fees.
  • Listening: about a tenth of a cent each time the app pays an artist. It's a fixed amount, not a percentage, and the app pays every few songs, so a month of listening costs a few cents in fees.

The artist receives every cent of their price. Amply takes nothing.

Getting your money back

In the Amply app, tap You, then Send back. Paste the Solana address of any wallet you control (in Phantom: Receive, choose Solana, copy), and send. Whatever you haven't spent goes back to you.

If you're an artist

  1. Make a wallet to be paid into. In your editor, open Settings → Who gets paid and tap Make my wallet with Face ID or a passkey. Your address is filled in for you. If you'd rather use a wallet app, install Phantom, then in Phantom tap Receive, choose Solana, copy the address, and paste it in. You don't need to buy anything to be paid.
  2. Publish. Payments arrive in your wallet as people listen.
  3. Cash out when you like. Your editor's Earnings tab shows how: send from your wallet to Cash App (US) or Coinbase, which turn it into dollars for your bank. If you made your wallet with a passkey, sending from the editor is coming; until then, Show my wallet key opens it in Phantom, where you send from (with a few cents of SOL for the network fee).

More on wallets and on getting money into your bank is in Which wallet should I use? To let fans subscribe by card as well, see Card subscriptions with Stripe.

Staying safe

  • Your key is the money. A passkey, a backup code or a wallet app's recovery words: nobody from Amply, Phantom or anywhere else will ever need them. Anyone who asks is trying to steal from you.
  • Always Solana. If an app or exchange asks which network to send on, the answer is Solana. USDC sent any other way won't arrive, and usually can't be recovered.
  • Keep it small. Treat the Amply wallet like cash in a pocket: a few dollars at a time. The app will never send more than $2 in one payment or $10 in a day, whatever an artist's page asks for.
