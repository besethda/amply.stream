Paying for music

How paying works

You put a few dollars in the Amply app. As you listen, it pays the artist a fraction of a cent for each minute, straight to them. Nobody takes a cut.

In one minute

  • What it costs: each artist sets their own price. A typical one is half a cent a minute, so an hour a day of listening comes to about $9 a month in total. You see each price before anything is charged.
  • How it's paid: automatically, every few songs, for exactly the minutes you listened. There's nothing to approve each time.
  • A safety cap: however an artist prices things, the app never sends more than $2 at once or $10 in a day.
  • Prefer a card? Many artists also offer a plain subscription: tap Subscribe on their page and pay with your card. Nothing to set up.
  • Buying to keep: some artists also sell songs and albums. Tap Buy on their page.
  • Free tracks play for anyone.

Setting it up

About five minutes, once (a little longer if you don't have Coinbase or Cash App yet).

  1. Make your wallet. In the Amply app, tap You, then Make my wallet with Face ID. (Your wallet is simply your Amply balance: where your listening money sits.) Your Face ID or fingerprint (on a computer, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your phone) is how you get it back on another device. On this one it stays ready to pay, so keep your phone locked and the balance small.
  2. Add money. Tap Top up and pick what you already use: Coinbase (most countries), or Cash App or a card (US). The app walks you through it and shows any fee first.
  3. Listen. Add an artist by their link or their website, and play.
More detail: fees, card top-ups in Europe, and other wallets
  • Network fees. Each payment also has a tiny network fee, a fraction of a cent. Many artists' services pay it for you. If one doesn't, the app asks you to add $1 for fees, once; that covers thousands of payments.
  • Top-up fees. Coinbase charges about 0.5% from a bank and 2.5% by card. Cash App is free at the moment. Topping up less often, in larger amounts, costs less.
  • Card top-ups in Europe aren't available yet; use Coinbase.
  • Already use a crypto wallet like Phantom? Tap Top up, then Already use a crypto wallet? Your money is digital dollars (USDC) on the Solana network, so send USDC, choosing Solana.
  • No Face ID on your device? Choose Use a backup code instead, and keep the code somewhere safe, like a password manager.

Getting your money back

Whatever you haven't spent is yours. In the app, tap You, then Send back, and follow the steps. Sending money out of the app needs a few cents of a coin called SOL for the network fee; the app tells you if you need to add some.

Staying safe

  • Nobody will ever need your Face ID, backup code or key. Not Amply, not an artist. Anyone who asks is trying to steal from you.
  • Keep it small, like cash in a pocket: a few dollars at a time.
  • Payments can't be reversed, so the app shows each artist's price before it pays anything.
