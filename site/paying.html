Paying for music

Never used crypto? Start here.

Artists on Amply are paid as you listen, straight from your wallet to theirs, with nobody taking a cut. Setting that up takes about ten minutes, once.

Two ways to pay an artist

Each artist chooses, and their page in the app says which:

  • By subscription — pay by card for a year or a few months. No crypto needed: tap Subscribe on their page, save the backup key the app shows you, and pay on Stripe's page. You can stop reading here.
  • By the minute — the app pays a fraction of a cent for each minute you listen, from a small wallet on your phone. That needs the setup below, once.

Free tracks play for anyone, either way.

Setting up paying by the minute

You'll use two apps: Phantom, a free wallet app where you buy the money, and Amply, which pays artists from its own small wallet while you listen. You move a few dollars from one to the other, and that's it.

  1. Install Phantom on your phone, from the link on phantom.com so you get the real app — fake wallet apps exist. Create a wallet, and write the list of words it shows you on paper. Anyone with those words can take what's in it; never type them anywhere else.
  2. Buy some USDC and a little SOL in Phantom. Tap Buy and pay by card. USDC is a digital dollar — it's what pays artists; $5 to $20 is plenty to start. SOL pays the network's fee, which is a fraction of a cent each time; about a dollar's worth lasts a long time. Choose USDC itself if Phantom offers it — buying SOL and swapping it costs an extra fee of about 1%. The card company will ask for ID. That's normal, and required by law.
    If your money shows up as "Cash" (a "$" coin): that's Phantom's own dollar, which Amply can't use yet. Tap Swap and turn it into USDC, plus a couple of dollars' worth of SOL. The same goes if Phantom only offered you SOL: swap most of it into USDC.
  3. Open the Amply app at amply.stream/app, go to Paying, and create a wallet. Write down its backup key too — it's the key to the money in this wallet.
  4. Top it up. The first time, send by hand: under Topping it up, copy the address, then in Phantom tap Send → SOL → paste → $1 worth, and again Send → USDC → paste → a few dollars. A brand-new wallet has to be set up on the network before anything else can reach it, and Phantom's ordinary Send does that. From then on it's one tap: tap an amount, and Phantom opens with everything filled in.
  5. Listen. Add an artist by their link or their website. You'll see their price before anything is charged, and the app pays them as you listen — no approving each payment.

Setting up on a computer? The top-up screen also shows codes you can scan with Phantom's camera instead of tapping.

What it costs you

  • Buying: the card company's fee, usually 3–5% including its exchange rate. Paying by bank transfer, where offered, is cheaper. Topping up $20 once costs less than $5 four times.
  • Setting up: about 30¢, once, to set up the Amply wallet on the network. It comes out of the SOL.
  • Listening: about a tenth of a cent each time the app pays an artist — a fixed amount, not a percentage, so it's around 1% at a typical 10¢ payment. Ten hours of listening in a month costs about 5¢ in fees.

The artist receives every cent of their price. Amply takes nothing.

Getting your money back

In the Amply app, under Sending it back, paste your Phantom address — in Phantom tap Receive, choose Solana, and copy it — and tap Send everything. Whatever you haven't spent goes back to Phantom.

If you're an artist

  1. Install Phantom and create a wallet, as above. Write down the words. You don't need to buy anything to be paid.
  2. Give Amply your address. In your editor, open Settings → Who gets paid. On a computer with the Phantom browser extension, tap Use my Phantom address; otherwise, in Phantom tap Receive, choose Solana, copy it, and paste it in.
  3. Publish. Payments arrive in Phantom as people listen.

More on choosing a wallet, and on getting money into your bank, is in Which wallet should I use? To let fans subscribe by card as well, see Card subscriptions with Stripe.

Staying safe

  • The words are the money. Nobody from Amply, Phantom or anywhere else will ever need them. Anyone who asks is trying to steal from you.
  • Always Solana. If an app or exchange asks which network to send on, the answer is Solana. USDC sent any other way won't arrive, and usually can't be recovered.
  • Keep it small. Treat the Amply wallet like cash in a pocket: a few dollars at a time. The app will never send more than $2 in one payment or $10 in a day, whatever an artist's page asks for.
