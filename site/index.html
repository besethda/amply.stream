amply

Artists host their own music.

In development

Set your own price. Get paid directly by the people who listen. Amply takes 0% — not a reduced rate, but zero, because there is no mechanism by which it could take anything.

Your music lives on hosting you own and log into. There is no catalogue, no algorithm, and no company in the middle. Nothing here can be sold, acquired, or shut down out from under you.

Status

Early, and not yet open for artists. The manifest specification and the node are written; onboarding, the management tools and the listener app are not. Follow along in the repository.
